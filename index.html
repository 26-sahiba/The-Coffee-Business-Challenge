About Starbucks in the Indian café market
Starbucks operates in India's organised premium café and coffeehouse space through Tata Starbucks, a joint venture between Tata Consumer Products and Starbucks Corporation. It competes with established international café brands such as Costa Coffee and Tim Hortons, as well as home-grown chains including Third Wave Coffee and Blue Tokai. Rather than competing only on the price of a cup of coffee, Starbucks differentiates itself through its beverage range, store experience, brand identity, customisation and convenient locations. This simulation explores how pricing, customer preferences, operating costs and competitors' decisions influence its performance in this market.

Each segment has its own constant elasticity es, so its profit-maximising price is Ps* = MC·es/(es−1). A segment with e ≤ 1 has no interior optimum, so its price goes to the ceiling. Capacity is rationed to the higher-priced segment first.
Real-world price discrimination requires customer groups to have different willingness to pay and limits on resale. A student offer or loyalty offer can be discussed as an example, but the prices here are hypothetical.
